Small-caps label above titles, on room cards and in lists; prefix Roman numerals for sections and courses.

```jsx
<Eyebrow numeral="IV">The Rooms of the House</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="dark">Est. 1823 · Paikpara, Kolkata</Eyebrow>
```

- `tone`: light (terracotta) · dark (copper, on terracotta-deep only) · terracotta (parchment — copper fails contrast on terracotta).
