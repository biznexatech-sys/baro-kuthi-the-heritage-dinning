An evening of your own in the study, for up to ten guests. The menu is chosen with the host beforehand.

Birthdays, anniversaries and annaprashan, hosted in the courtyard or the Jalsaghar, in the old manner.

Midday tables for colleagues and guests, with a set menu from either table.
  1. I

    Telephone the host

    Tell the host the date, the number of guests and the occasion.

  2. II

    Settle the room and menu

    The host suggests a room and a menu from either table.

  3. III

    Arrive and be received

    The house is prepared, and your guests are received at the gate.
